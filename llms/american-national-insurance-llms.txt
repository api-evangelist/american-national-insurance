# American National Insurance

> American National Insurance Company is a multi-line insurance holding company founded in 1905
> and headquartered in Galveston, Texas. It writes life insurance, annuities, pension risk
> transfer, supplemental health, and property & casualty coverage through subsidiary companies
> operating in all 50 states, distributed by independent agents, financial advisors and
> broker-dealers. It was acquired by Brookfield Reinsurance in 2022.

## API posture

American National publishes **no public API, no developer portal, and no machine-readable
contract**. Probed 2026-09-02:

- No `/llms.txt`, `/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/developers` or `/developer`
  on www.americannational.com (all HTTP 404).
- No document at any `/.well-known/` path on any American National host (see
  `well-known/american-national-insurance-well-known.yml`).
- No A2A agent card, no MCP server, no GraphQL surface, no published WSDL.
- Two IBM API Connect / DataPower gateways are live and reachable —
  `api.americannational.com` and `apigw.americannational.com/american-national/prod` — but they
  are private back ends for American National's own client portal and mobile app. Every path
  returns an HTTP 500 SOAP fault or a JSON 404; no product, contract or reference is published
  for them. They are not an offered API product and are not listed as APIs in this profile.

Do not attempt to call these gateways. There is no public onboarding path, no documented
authentication scheme, and no terms permitting third-party integration.

## Products

- Life insurance — term, whole life and universal life through American National subsidiaries.
- Annuities — fixed, fixed indexed and income annuities for retirement income.
- Pension risk transfer — group annuity products for defined benefit plan sponsors.
- Health insurance — supplemental health, Medicare supplement and group health.
- Farm and ranch property & casualty, distributed under the Farm Family brand.
